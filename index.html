Mimi Memo

A tinnitus diary for your phone

Mimi Memo lets you log episodes in one tap. Track loudness, sound character, which ear, and what was going on around you. Review patterns over time and bring something concrete to your next appointment.

Free, private, no account needed.

Open the app

What it does

  • Log severity, sound character, and which ear
  • Track context like stress, sleep, and surrounding noise
  • Review charts showing frequency, time of day, and day of week patterns

Who it's for

Anyone living with tinnitus who wants a simple, private way to keep records, especially if you're working with a doctor or audiologist and want something concrete to refer to.

Install on your phone

Mimi Memo works like an app but doesn't need an app store.

iPhone

  1. Open app.mimimemo.com in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (the box with an arrow pointing up)
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add

Android

  1. Open app.mimimemo.com in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right)
  3. Tap Add to Home screen
  4. Tap Add
